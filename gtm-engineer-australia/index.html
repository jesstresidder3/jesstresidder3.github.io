01

Build the engine

The complete outbound infrastructure, strategy, systems and live campaigns, built inside your business.

Step 01

Define your ideal buyers, markets and buying signals

A deep dive workshop defines the foundation of your outbound: the exact roles and company types that make a qualified prospect, the full addressable market that fits, and the buying signals that show a company is ready for a conversation now, from job postings and funding to leadership changes. That targeting layer sets when outreach goes out, so a prospect hears from you at the point they have a reason to care.

  • Ideal client profile defined by industry, size and role
  • Total addressable market mapped and sized
  • Buying signal library built for your market

What you receive: a complete targeting blueprint, documented and yours.

Step 02

Build sending infrastructure you own

In parallel with strategy, we provision dedicated sending domains and mailboxes under your control, fully authenticated with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, and warmed before a single campaign launches. Your primary business domain is never used for cold outreach, so it stays protected throughout.

  • Separate sending domains and inboxes, registered in your name
  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC authentication in place
  • Structured warmup before any outreach begins

What you receive: warmed, documented sending infrastructure on your domains.

Step 03

Every prospect gets a message built for them

We segment your market across industry, role and buying signal, and write copy for each meaningful combination. Where a segment has a live signal, the email opens with the trigger that makes the timing obvious, and the rest open with the core value proposition for their industry and role. The result is a complete copy library covering every segment.

  • Market segmented by industry, role and signal
  • Copy variants written and tailored per segment
  • Messaging reviewed and approved by you before anything sends

What you receive: a full copy library plus the framework behind it.

Step 04

Signal tracking goes live, and campaigns launch

The signal tracking system monitors your market continuously, and when a prospect shows intent they are routed automatically into the sequence built for their segment. Everything connects to the warmed infrastructure, we run final quality checks, and campaigns go live, syncing replies and meetings to your CRM as they happen.

  • Signal tracking live across your defined triggers
  • Verified, enriched contacts routed into the right sequences
  • Campaigns launched across email and LinkedIn, monitored from the first send

What you receive: a live, running outbound operation connected to your CRM.

02

Scale and refine

With campaigns live, we refine against real data and build out the full pipeline management and sales intelligence layer.

Step 01

Refine outbound against real data

Every campaign generates data: which segments respond, which signals produce the best conversations and which angles get replies. We refine messaging, adjust segments, add or retire signals, and replace copy that underperforms. The system sharpens week by week.

  • Messaging and targeting refined on a continuous basis
  • Copy that stops getting replies is pulled and the winners get more volume
  • Documented performance benchmarks per segment
Step 02

Manage the pipeline from reply to closed deal

The infrastructure that carries a prospect from first reply to a signed engagement takes shape. CRM workflows are configured so every opportunity has a clear status, a defined next step and full context. Nothing sits in an inbox, and nothing falls between handoffs.

  • CRM workflows with clear stages and next steps
  • Replies and meetings sync to the CRM as they happen
  • Handoff procedures so no opportunity is dropped
Step 03

Turn every touchpoint into sales intelligence

Your outbound operation starts generating insight at every step. A brief is prepared before every meeting, covering background, company context, relevant signals and prior interactions. Calls are recorded, transcribed and logged against the prospect record, so context is always available.

  • A brief prepared before every meeting
  • Calls recorded, transcribed and logged to the prospect record
  • Full context available to your team long after the call
Step 04

See the full picture every week

Weekly reports cover open rates, reply rates, positive replies, meetings booked, pipeline generated and the status of every campaign. You always see what is working, what is changing and what we are doing about it.

  • Weekly performance reports on the metrics that matter
  • Pipeline visibility for every campaign

What you receive: a refined engine, a configured pipeline system and a live sales intelligence layer.
