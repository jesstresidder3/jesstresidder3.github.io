Tools I work with

What this looks like in practice

Phase 01

Build and launch

You come out of this phase with a documented targeting blueprint, warmed sending infrastructure on your own domains, a full copy library and campaigns running.

01

Targeting

One deep dive sets the foundation: the roles and company types that make a qualified prospect, the market that fits, and the signals that show a company is ready to talk now.

  • Ideal client profile by industry, size and role
  • Total addressable market mapped and sized
  • Buying signal library built for your market
02

Infrastructure

Dedicated sending domains and mailboxes under your control, authenticated and warmed before a single campaign launches, so your main business domain stays protected.

  • Separate domains and inboxes, registered in your name
  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC authentication in place
  • Structured warmup before any outreach begins
03

Copy

Your market gets segmented by industry, role and signal, with copy written for each combination. Where a segment has a live signal, the email opens with that trigger.

  • Market segmented by industry, role and signal
  • Copy variants written per segment
  • Everything approved by you before it sends
04

Launch

Signal tracking watches your market, and a prospect who shows intent gets routed into the sequence built for their segment.

  • Signal tracking live across your triggers
  • Verified, enriched contacts in the right sequences
  • Email and LinkedIn campaigns running
Phase 02

Refine and scale

You come out of this phase with an engine that sharpens every week, a pipeline system your team works from and a sales intelligence layer feeding every call.

05

Refinement

Every campaign generates data on which segments reply and which angles work. Messaging, segments and signals get adjusted, and copy that underperforms gets replaced.

  • Messaging and targeting refined continuously
  • Copy that stops getting replies is pulled
  • Documented benchmarks per segment
06

Pipeline

CRM workflows carry a prospect from first reply to signed engagement, so every opportunity has a status, a next step and full context.

  • Clear stages and next steps in your CRM
  • Replies and meetings sync as they happen
  • Handoffs defined so nothing gets dropped
07

Sales intelligence

A brief is prepared before every meeting, and calls are recorded, transcribed and logged against the prospect record.

  • A brief before every meeting
  • Calls transcribed onto the prospect record
  • Full context available to your team later
08

Reporting

Weekly reporting on open rates, replies, meetings booked and pipeline generated, plus the status of every campaign.

  • Weekly performance reporting
  • Pipeline visibility for every campaign
  • You always see what changed and why
